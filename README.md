# Kingdom Sim
A Minecraft mod (NeoForge 1.21.1): a living realm. One kingdom holds back a tide of monster nations on a web of towns, sites, strongholds and capitals, with a turn every Minecraft day, raids rolled or fought live, real damage that towns rebuild from blueprints, and town guards. Part of the [Dungeon Delving](https://github.com/Dunedain-Ranger-Dev/dungeon-delving) modpack.

It needs [Dungeon Delving](https://github.com/Dunedain-Ranger-Dev/dungeon-delving): its dungeon factions are the monster nations, raiding with its mobs and led by its bosses. With Professions and Skills, guards and area-levelled monsters show their levels; with Iron's Spells, towns get battlemages; with FTB Chunks, the realm shows on its map.

The design is in [docs/design](docs/design), starting with [realm.md](docs/design/realm.md).
